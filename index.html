I am a Lecturer at the College of Computer Science and Technology, Zhejiang Normal University, which I joined in May 2026. I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology from Zhejiang University, advised by Prof. Shuiguang Deng. I am originally from Zhengzhou, Henan, China.

My research focuses on semi-supervised learning, long-tailed representation learning, and embodied intelligence. I am interested in learning reliable representations from limited labels and imbalanced data, and in bringing these methods to real-world perception and interaction.

My work has appeared at CVPR and ACM Multimedia. I also collaborate with hospitals affiliated with Zhejiang University on multimodal AI for healthcare, with publications in Cell Reports Medicine and iScience and applications in smart healthcare.

Research interests: Semi-supervised learning; Long-tailed representation learning; Embodied intelligence; Medical AI.
